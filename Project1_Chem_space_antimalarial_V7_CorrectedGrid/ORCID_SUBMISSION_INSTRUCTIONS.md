## Important Note About ORCID iDs in ACS LaTeX Submissions

The `achemso` LaTeX document class used for ACS journals **does not support** the `\orcid{}` command in the manuscript source files. Instead, ORCID iDs are entered directly in the **Paragon Plus online submission system** during the manuscript upload process.

This is standard procedure for ACS journal submissions and is documented in the ACS Author Guidelines.

## How to Enter ORCID iDs in Paragon Plus

### During Manuscript Submission

When you submit your manuscript to https://paragonplus.acs.org/, you will reach a screen titled **"Add/Edit/Remove Author(s)"**. For each author:

1. **Click "Add Author" or "Edit"** for the author entry

2. **Locate the ORCID iD field** (it will be clearly labeled)

3. **For Myke Vital Sao Temgoua (corresponding author):**
   - Enter: `0009-0004-5170-2309`
   - Or paste the full URL: `https://orcid.org/0009-0004-5170-2309`
   - The system will validate the format automatically

4. **For co-authors without ORCID iDs:**
   - Leave the ORCID field blank
   - The system will allow submission with blank ORCIDs for co-authors
   - Co-authors can be invited to add their ORCIDs later through the system

5. **Click "Save" or "Continue"**

## Why ORCID iDs Are Important

1. **Author Disambiguation:** Ensures your work is correctly attributed to you
2. **Research Visibility:** Links all your publications across different journals
3. **ACS Policy:** Strongly encouraged (and soon to be required) for all ACS submissions
4. **Funding Requirements:** Many funding agencies now require ORCID iDs

## Co-Author ORCID Registration (Optional)

If your co-authors want to create ORCID iDs before or after submission:

1. **Go to:** https://orcid.org/register
2. **Fill out the form:** Name, email, password
3. **Verify email:** Click the link sent to their email
4. **Copy their 16-digit ORCID iD**
5. **Add to Paragon Plus:** They can be invited to add it to the manuscript

**Time required:** ~2 minutes per person
