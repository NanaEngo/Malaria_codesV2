## Important Note About ORCID iDs in ACS LaTeX Submissions

The `achemso` LaTeX document class used for ACS journals **does not support** the `\orcid{}` command in the manuscript source files. Instead, ORCID iDs are entered directly in the **Paragon Plus online submission system** during the manuscript upload process.

This is standard procedure for ACS journal submissions and is documented in the ACS Author Guidelines.
