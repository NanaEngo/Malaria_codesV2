\opening{Dear Editors,}

\noindent 16 September 2026

We submit the manuscript entitled \textbf{``Target breadth and mutation resilience in African-natural-product-inspired antimalarial chemotypes: docking-based resistance-resilience scoring''} for consideration as a Full Paper in \textit{Digital Discovery}.

Drug-resistant malaria demands chemotypes that survive the pathogen's evolution, yet most virtual screening cascades rank compounds against a single static target snapshot. Our contribution is a high-throughput computational triage methodology, not another candidate list. From a scaffold-guided expansion of African natural product and synthetic antimalarial seeds into a \num{65856}-compound hybrid library (\qty{92.6}{\percent} whole-molecule novelty with \qty{69.3}{\percent} privileged-scaffold retention), we formulate a Resistance-aware Retention Score (RRS) that quantifies per-target affinity retention across wild-type and clinically observed mutant states of four \textit{Plasmodium falciparum} targets (PfDHFR, PfCRT, PfATP4, PfClpP). Multi-parameter optimisation distils the library to 17 mutation-resilient candidates, each validated by an external data-first chain: DEKOIS 2.0 benchmarking, MMV compound enrichment, and sub-\qty{2.0}{\angstrom} redocking RMSD on every target.

This fits your scope on three counts: (i) a high-throughput computational methodology for molecular design, (ii) an advanced data workflow with full provenance, checksums, and machine-readable tables, and (iii) reproducible, reusable outputs for the community.

\textbf{Data and code:} the complete deposit --- data, code, raw docking outputs, provenance records, checksum manifest --- is public on Zenodo under an MIT licence at \url{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22696778}. Editors and referees have immediate access; nothing is embargoed.

The work is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, has been approved by all authors, and there are no conflicts to declare.

\closing{We thank you for your consideration.}
