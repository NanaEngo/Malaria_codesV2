Invitation letter
Dear Prof./Dr./Mr./Ms: Sao Temgoua Myke Vital,
We are delighted to invite you to participate as a speaker in the Workshop on New era of physics to address local needs, which will be held at the University of Ngaoundere from 23rd to 24t* September 2026.
The workshop is funded by the Stellenbosch Institute for Advanced Study (STIAS) in the framework of the Iso Lomso ('eye of tomorrow') program. Launched in 2017, the Iso Lomso Program aims to support emerging African researchers in the early stages of their careers and to assist them in becoming established scholars in their respective fields.
As part of this support, STIAS provides Iso Lomso Fellows with a range of opportunities, including funding for the organization of research workshops either at STIAS in Stellenbosch or at their home institutions. The workshop at the University of Ngaoundere is being organized within this framework and in partnership with the University of Ngaoundere.
The two-day workshop will feature sessions focusing on Al-inspired physics research, Al-assisted cryptography and computer science, and Al-driven research in quantum optics, quantum computing, and quantum chemistry. It will bring together researchers from Cameroonian universities, guest researchers from neighboring countries, including Chad and the Central African Republic, as well as representatives from STIAS.
The workshop aims to provide a platform for researchers to exchange ideas, explore common research interests, and establish a collaborative research network across the participating countries. Through these interactions, the workshop seeks to foster future collaborations and address shared research challenges and local needs through innovative approaches in physics and related fields. The program will cover your accommodation and living expenses, as well as provide support towards your international and local transportation costs associated with participation in the workshop. We look forward to hosting you for the workshop at the University of Ngaoundere.
