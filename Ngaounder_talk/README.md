## Abstract Overview

The abstract presents a unified narrative of your antimalarial drug discovery pipeline focusing on completed work from **P1, P2, P3, and P5 only** (P4, P6, and P7 excluded as requested). The narrative flows without explicit project labels.

### Key Results Highlighted

**Chemical Space Exploration and Virtual Screening**
- 65,856 molecules, 92.6% novelty
- 20 top candidates (MPO 0.515–0.550)
- DEKOIS 2.0 validation, redocking RMSD < 2.0 Å
- Submitted to *J. Chem. Inf. Model.*

**Resistance-Aware Polypharmacology and Molecular Dynamics**
- 87.5% estimand divergence (static vs. MD)
- 16-system MD pilot complete (10 ns each)
- 100% GNINA CNN agreement on Class-A candidates
- African natural product chemical space: *Fsp³* = 0.22, *QED* = 0.70, *MPO* = 0.728
- Prepared for *JCIM* submission

**Quantum-Inspired Representations**
- **Honest-negative result:** ECFP4 0.9475 > quantum-inspired 0.8876
- No quantum advantage over classical baselines (p = 0.374)
- Establishes rigorous reporting standards

**Deep Learning Under Distribution Shift**
- **Honest-negative result:** ECFP4-RF 0.8300 > all GNN/Transformer models
- Fingerprint advantage concentrates in out-of-distribution tail
- External validation on 22,267 ChEMBL compounds confirms ordering

## Key Methodological Contributions

1. **Honest-Negative Reporting**
   - Transparent documentation when advanced methods don't outperform baselines
   - Established across quantum-inspired (P3) and deep learning (P5) studies

2. **Estimand Divergence Framing**
   - Method disagreement as a positive triage filter (87.5% static vs. MD divergence)
   - Computational disagreement informs prioritization rather than indicating failure

3. **Regional Context**
   - African natural product chemical space characterization
   - WHO resistance profiles for Central African isolates
