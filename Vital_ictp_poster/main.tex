  \begin{block}{Background \& Motivation}

  \textbf{Malaria} remains a devastating disease, particularly in tropical and subtropical regions, with over \num{247000000} cases and approximately \num{619000} deaths reported in 2023. 

\begin{itemize}
    \item[\textbf{$\Rightarrow$}] \textbf{Challenge:} Resistance to existing drugs is rapidly increasing due to mutations in \textit{Plasmodium falciparum} strains. Conventional virtual screening requires tens of thousands of docking runs per target (exceeding \num{1000} CPU-hours), limiting antimalarial discovery in resource-constrained malaria-endemic regions where \qty{94}{\percent} of global disease burden occurs.
    \item[\textbf{$\Rightarrow$}] \textbf{Opportunity:} African natural products offer a rich, underexplored source of bioactive scaffolds, but screening and optimization remain major bottlenecks.
\end{itemize}

\begin{center}
\textbf{\textit{Can we reduce computational costs by 99\% while maintaining predictive accuracy?}}
\end{center}

  \end{block}

  \begin{block}{Natural Products in Drugs Discovery}

  \begin{alertblock}{Key Objectives}

    \begin{itemize}
      \item \textbf{Efficient screening strategy}: VAE-based centroid sampling to reduce screening from \num{65856} compounds to \num{484} representatives (\qty{99.3}{\percent} cost reduction)
      \item \textbf{Dual-filter consensus docking}: Combine DiffDock and AutoDock Vina across four \textit{P. falciparum} resistance targets (DHFR, CRT, ClpP, ATP4)
      \item \textbf{Multi-parameter optimization (MPO)}: Integrate binding affinity, drug-likeness (QED), and ADMET properties for candidate ranking
      \item \textbf{External validation}: Benchmark against MMV Malaria Box (\num{399} confirmed actives) and DEKOIS 2.0 decoy set
      \item \textbf{Scaffold novelty analysis}: Quantify chemical space exploration beyond simple interpolation
    \end{itemize}

  \end{alertblock}

  \begin{alertblock}{Key Results}

    \begin{itemize}
      \item \textbf{Computational efficiency}: \qty{99.3}{\percent} reduction in screening cost (\num{65856} $\to$ \num{484} centroids) while maintaining high discriminatory power (consensus-score ROC-AUC \numrange{0.924}{1.000})
      
      \item \textbf{Scaffold novelty}: \qty{92.6}{\percent} of generated molecules are ECFP4-unreachable from seed library (Tanimoto $<$ \num{0.4}), yet \qty{69.3}{\percent} scaffold recovery demonstrates preservation of bioactive frameworks
      
      \item \textbf{Synthesizable leads}: \num{19913} candidates with favorable properties: \qty{94.1}{\percent} Lipinski-compliant, \qty{100}{\percent} selectively antiparasitic (SI $>$ \num{10}, $n=810$ screened), \qty{57.0}{\percent} synthetically accessible (SYBA $>$ \num{0}$)
      
      \item \textbf{External validation}: MMV Malaria Box benchmark confirms enrichment capacity (EF\qty{5}{\percent} and BEDROC $\alpha=20$)
      
      \item \textbf{Open science}: Complete \num{65856}-molecule hybrid library deposited (Zenodo DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.19608875)
        \end{itemize}

  \end{alertblock}
