\opening{Dear Editors,}

We are pleased to submit our manuscript, \textbf{``Quantum-Inspired Molecular Representations for African Natural Product Chemical Space: Topological Fingerprints, Tensor Network Embeddings, and Quantum Kernel Scores''}, for consideration as an \textbf{Original Research Article} in the \textit{Journal of Cheminformatics}.

This work introduces three novel molecular descriptors---Topological Fingerprint (TFP), Tensor Network Embedding (TNE), and Quantum Kernel Score (QKS)---benchmarked against classical fingerprints on 19,849 African NP-derived candidates. We demonstrate that persistent homology resolves the scaffold paradox in generative AI (92.6\% Tanimoto novelty with 69.3\% scaffold recovery), TNE achieves 15.6$\times$ compression on a standard workstation, and quantum kernels match classical baselines after proper hyperparameter tuning (QKS AUC 0.751 vs RBF 0.701, $p=0.088$, ns). We report honest negative results: classical ECFP4 remains superior for primary screening (AUC 0.868 vs hybrid 0.842, $p=0.111$).

All data and code are deposited on Zenodo and GitHub under the MIT licence. This work is original, not under consideration elsewhere, and all authors have approved the submission.
