## Specific Changes Made

### Text Optimizations (Reduced ~35% overall)

1. **Numbers abbreviated:** 247 million → 247M, 619,000 → 619K
2. **Technical terms shortened:** ROC-AUC → AUC, Calinski-Harabasz → CH
3. **Redundant words removed:** "confirmed", "established", parenthetical explanations
4. **Lists to inline:** Bullet points → inline text with • separators
5. **Concise phrasing:** "endemic regions where 94% of cases occur" → "endemic regions (94% cases)"

### Spacing Optimizations

1. **All vertical spacing:** 0.5em → 0.3em (saved ~105pt total)

4. **References font:** footnotesize → scriptsize
5. **Impact block:** Added `\small` directive
