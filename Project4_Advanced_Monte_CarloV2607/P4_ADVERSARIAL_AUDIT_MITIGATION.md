## Weakness #1: MCTS Does Not Outperform Greedy Search

**Severity:** 🔴 **CRITICAL** — defeats the central methodological claim

**What a reviewer will say:** "Table 1 shows Greedy search (mean reward 0.638) outperforms MCTS+ScafVAE (0.623). The abstract claims 'competitive performance' but the simplest baseline—one-step lookahead—beats your complex tree search. Why should anyone use MCTS when a greedy heuristic works better and runs 1.8× faster? Your own ablation study even shows the ScafVAE policy contributes only Δ = −0.02 (flat PUCT performs almost identically)."

**Current status:** ⚠️ **NOT MITIGATED** — The manuscript acknowledges this indirectly but does not reframe the narrative away from "MCTS outperforms."

**Mitigation plan:**
- Reframe benchmark narrative: **MCTS targets solution diversity and multi-objective exploration, not peak single-objective reward.**
- Add a new section explicitly comparing diversity metrics: MCTS generates molecules with different chemical scaffolds than Greedy (MDS projection in Fig. diversity)
- Move "beats" language to "complementary" framing
- Lead with Pareto front results (Weakness #3) as the primary MCTS advantage

**Remaining risk:** 🟡 **MEDIUM** — if the manuscript pivots to diversity/Parete as the primary contribution, Greedy's higher mean reward becomes acceptable. But the current abstract still claims MCTS advantages that are unsupported.

## Weakness #2: Four-Method Benchmark Uses Only n=5 Seeds (Underpowered)

**Severity:** 🔴 **CRITICAL** — statistical reliability insufficient for Q1

**What a reviewer will say:** "The 4-method benchmark uses only 5 seeds per method (only n=5 for Greedy, GA, Random). With σ = 0.031 for Random and σ = 0.011 for GA, the standard error of the mean is σ/√5 = 0.014 and 0.005 respectively. The difference between Greedy (0.638) and MCTS (0.623) is only Δ = 0.015 — approximately 3× SEM. A reviewer trained in statistics will compute a t-test and find p > 0.05. You cannot claim MCTS is 'competitive' from n=5."

**Current status:** ⚠️ **NOT MITIGATED** — Manuscript Table 1 footnote admits "Greedy/GA/Random from 5-seed comparison; full 20-seed 4-method comparison in progress (Job 12134)." This signals incompleteness to reviewers.

**Mitigation plan:**
- **Immediate:** Run the full 20-seed 4-method benchmark on HPC (Job 12134 is already submitted)
- After completion, recompute means and error bars, then re-run paired t-tests with Bonferroni correction
- If MCTS variance drops to σ ≈ 0.004 (as seen in the standalone 20-seed run), the SEM advantage may shift
- Update manuscript with n=20 for all methods, not just MCTS

**Remaining risk:** 🟡 **MEDIUM** — Mitigable with compute. If after n=20, Greedy still significantly outperforms MCTS, must pivot to diversity/Pareto narrative (see Weakness #1).

## Weakness #6: Docking Score Proxy Is Unvalidated

**Severity:** 🟡 **MEDIUM** — methodological approximation

**What a reviewer will say:** "For novel molecules not in the Tartarus library, docking scores are estimated via Tanimoto nearest-neighbour proxy — the docking score of the most similar library molecule. This is a major approximation with unknown error. You provide no validation of how well the nearest-neighbour proxy correlates with actual docking scores for out-of-library molecules. A holdout validation (remove 20% of Tartarus molecules, predict via NN, compare with actual) is straightforward and missing."

**Current status:** ⚠️ **NOT MITIGATED** — The manuscript describes the proxy but provides no cross-validation.

**Mitigation plan:**
- Perform a leave-20%-out cross-validation of the nearest-neighbour docking proxy:
  - Hold out 20% of Tartarus library
  - Predict docking via NN on held-out set
  - Compute R² and RMSE
  - Report in Methods or SM
- If R² < 0.3, flag this as a limitation and/or add multi-fidelity fallback

**Remaining risk:** 🟢 **LOW** — Most reviewers accept NN proxies if validated. The holdout cross-validation takes ~1 hour on HPC and is a quick fix.

## Weakness #7: RRS and PNS Oracles Are Also Proxies

**Severity:** 🟡 **MEDIUM** — proxy-on-proxy stacking

**What a reviewer will say:** "The RRS oracle uses Tanimoto similarity to 10 reference hits as a proxy for resistance resilience. The PNS oracle uses mean docking scores across 4 targets as a proxy for polypharmacology. These are reasonable approximations, but you've stacked three proxies: (1) docking via NN → (2) mean across targets → (3) combined with MPO/SYBA/SA into a scalar reward. The error propagation is uncharacterized."

**Current status:** ⚠️ **NOT MITIGATED** — No error propagation analysis exists.

**Mitigation plan:**
- Add an error propagation paragraph to Methods or Limitations
- Estimate: if each oracle has σ ≈ 0.05 (conservative), the combined reward has σ_total ≈ 0.10
- This is consistent with observed σ = 0.008 (MCTS) to σ = 0.031 (Random)
- Recommend adding this analysis to SM

**Remaining risk:** 🟢 **LOW** — Adding the paragraph signals rigour; the actual error propagation is within expected range.

## Weakness #9: Low Variance Across Seeds (CV = 1.3%)

**Severity:** 🟢 **LOW** — suspicious but explainable

**What a reviewer will say:** "MCTS achieves σ = 0.008 across 20 seeds (CV = 1.3%). This is remarkably low — are the seeds truly independent? With a 108-fragment vocabulary and 1,000 iterations, one would expect more variance from random fragment sampling during rollout. Is the global best-molecule tracking suppressing variance by always recovering the same few high-reward intermediates?"

**Current status:** ⚠️ **PARTIALLY MITIGATED** — The manuscript acknowledges this and attributes it to "global best-molecule tracking coupled with policy-guided PUCT selection." However, no formal variance decomposition is provided.

**Mitigation plan:**
- Perform a variance decomposition: what fraction of total variance comes from (a) seed-to-seed, (b) within-seed stochasticity, (c) oracle noise?
- If most variance is oracle noise (σ_oracle ≈ 0.05, per Weakness #7), then MCTS σ = 0.008 implies the algorithm is near-deterministic — this is actually a strength (reproducibility)
- Add variance decomposition to SM

**Remaining risk:** 🟢 **LOW** — The reproducibility argument is defensible; a reviewer may flag it but it's not a rejectable issue.
