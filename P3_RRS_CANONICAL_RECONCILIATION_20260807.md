## Decision
The canonical cross-paper H1–RRS result is the expanded cohort in:

- `results/p3_h1_rrs_correlation_final.csv`

## Canonical values

- Cohort: **n = 494** complete computational RRS + TFP profiles.
- H1 count: Spearman **rho = 0.2399**, **p = 6.7578e-08**.
- H1 count, partial Spearman controlling for MW, ring count, fraction sp3 and H0 count: **rho_partial = 0.0329**, **p = 0.4668**.
- Interpretation: the unadjusted association is weak and disappears after covariate control. It is **size-confounded and hypothesis-generating**, not evidence of an independent topological biomarker or clinical predictor.

## Superseded values

The earlier n = 77 / rho = 0.312 analysis and the n = 14 pilot are retained only as historical exploratory analyses in audit history. They are not canonical headline evidence and must not be used in the abstract, conclusion, README, or current acceptance assessment.

## Scope boundary

The 494-compound computational RRS/TFP cohort is distinct from the 17 set-C P2 polypharmacology leads and from the four named P1 leads used for MD. No MD or experimental validation is implied by this correlation analysis.
