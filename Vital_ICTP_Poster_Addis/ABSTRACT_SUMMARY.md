### Key Components Included:

**1. P1 - Chemical Space & Virtual Screening**
- 65,856 compound hybrid library (92.6% novel, 69.3% scaffold recovery)
- Structure-based virtual screening against 4 malaria targets
- 17 polypharmacology candidates with RRS classification (A*:6, B:5, C:5, D:1)
- 11.8% with high cross-target selectivity (ACSI > 0.70)

**2. P2 - Molecular Dynamics Validation**
- OpenFF 2.2.0/CHARMM36m validation protocol
- Stable binding trajectories across wild-type and mutant receptor states
- GPU-accelerated production (28 ns/day at 310 K)
- Resistance-aware computational framework

**3. P3 - Quantum-Inspired Representations**
- Benchmark comparison: ECFP4 (0.9475) vs. hybrid models (0.8876)
- Persistent homology and tensor network embeddings
- Scaffold-split validation on 19,836 compounds
- Honest-negative result: quantum descriptors complementary but not superior

**4. P4 - Pareto-Guided Monte Carlo**
- Multi-objective candidate generation (synthetic accessibility, resistance similarity, polypharmacology)
- Scalar benchmark: Random (0.6724) outperformed MCTS (0.6649)
- Transparent trade-off analysis via Pareto fronts

**5. P5 - Graph Neural Networks**
- ECFP4-RF superiority under scaffold split (0.8300 vs. GIN 0.8047)
- Topological fusion (GIN-TFP) provided modest gains (0.8138)
- ChemBERTa comparison across 19,836 antimalarial panel
