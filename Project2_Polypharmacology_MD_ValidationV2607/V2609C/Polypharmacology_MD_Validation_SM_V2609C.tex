\subsection*{Convergence and interaction analysis}
The canonical Set-C pilot comprises 16 MM--GBSA endpoints: 12 mutant systems and four WT baselines, each one production trajectory and 100 stored snapshots. SD$_{\mathrm{prop}}$ is the within-trajectory SD; SEM is frame-level only. Neither quantifies between-replicate uncertainty. The WT replicate is a diagnostic control, not part of the 16-system table; the K76A diagnostic replicate is reported separately below.

The difference is a diagnostic measure of inter-replicate sensitivity, not a confidence interval for affinity or a biological resistance effect.}
\label{tab:s19_k76a_replicate}

Canonical Set-C endpoints have SD$_{\mathrm{prop}}$ \qtyrange{1.17}{3.62}{\kcalmol} and frame-level SEM \qtyrange{0.16}{0.61}{\kcalmol}. Snapshots are temporally autocorrelated and each state has a single trajectory, so neither quantity quantifies between-replicate uncertainty — illustrated by the \qty{7.75}{\kcalmol} K76A inter-replicate difference. The WT replicate offset was \qty{2.01}{\kcalmol} (\cref{tab:setc_wt_replicate_consistency}), used only as a within-protocol endpoint diagnostic, not applied to MD distance ratios.

Interaction fingerprints (ProLIF 2.2.1, \num{100} frames per trajectory, sixteen systems) identify a sparse set of persistent contacts: four to eleven contacts per system exceed \qty{50}{\percent} occupancy, most at \qty{100}{\percent}. Recurring anchors include PfCRT TYR16 (fully occupied in five of six PfCRT systems) and PfDHFR LEU46/MET55, consistent with the canonical binding pockets. PP-15 was docked to wild-type PfCRT and PfDHFR (Vina 1.2.7) under the Set-C protocol. Both systems completed equilibration and \qty{10}{\ns} production (\cref{tab:s_pp15}) with bound fraction \num{1.000} (mean minimum distances \qty{2.77}{\angstrom} PfDHFR, \qty{3.20}{\angstrom} PfCRT). MM-GBSA (PBC-whole, GB\textsuperscript{OBC2} igb=5, \qty{0.15}{\molar} salt): \qty{-29.52 \pm 0.33}{\kcalmol} (PfDHFR) and \qty{-27.79 \pm 0.49}{\kcalmol} (PfCRT; \cref{tab:s_pp15}). No mutant or RRS estimate is reported; single-replicate endpoints are within-protocol feasibility contrasts. Five-seed redocking was stable within \qty{0.03}{\kcalmol} on both receptors (\cref{tab:s17_multiseed_redock}). The same control was repeated for PP-01 on the canonical wild-type inputs (\cref{tab:s17_multiseed_redock}): dispersion within \qty{0.05}{\kcalmol} on both receptors. The canonical PfDHFR WT score (\qty{-7.50}{\kcalmol}) falls inside the five-seed spread (mean \qty{-7.499 \pm 0.021}{\kcalmol}). The canonical PfCRT WT score (\qty{-9.30}{\kcalmol}) lies \qty{0.04}{\kcalmol} above the observed range (\qtyrange{-9.262}{-9.225}{\kcalmol}) because that single run's receptor-preparation detail is not preserved on disk; this offset is within the \qty{0.05}{\kcalmol} seed-dispersion bound, so the seed-stability conclusion is unchanged.
