\noindent Dear Prof.\ Merz,

We submit our manuscript entitled \textbf{``Estimand Divergence Between Static Docking and Molecular Dynamics as a Triage Filter for Antimalarial Leads''} for consideration as a research Article in the \textit{Journal of Chemical Information and Modeling}.

Overcoming multi-target drug resistance in \textit{Plasmodium falciparum} is a central goal in antimalarial lead discovery. Static docking scores capture unrelaxed grid complementarity, ignoring dynamic pocket relaxation upon mutation. We ask whether static grid docking and short explicit-solvent molecular dynamics estimate the same target quantity across a resistance-mutant panel, and whether their directional disagreement can flag states for prospective biochemical testing. We apply both procedures to 396 African natural products and 454 synthetic antimalarials across 136 wild-type and mutant structures of PfDHFR and PfCRT.

We define a Resistance-Resilience Score (RRS) for score retention across mutant panels, excluding non-binding wild-type baselines ($|S_{\mathrm{WT}}| < \qty{5.0}{\kcalmol}$). In short explicit-solvent MD (\qty{10}{\ns}), static scores and trajectory pose retention diverged directionally in \qty{87.5}{\percent} (7/8; 95\% Wilson CI \qtyrange{52.9}{97.8}{\percent}, $n=\num{8}$) of matched comparisons --- distinct physical estimands (unrelaxed grid complementarity vs.\ local dynamic accommodation), not docking failure. Per Soares et al.\ (2023), these short trajectories are secondary structural checks: a calibration signal, not a validated prediction.

The observation is bounded by three stress tests, two negative and reported as first-class findings: (i) Vina enrichment on DEKOIS 2.0 \textit{Pf}DHFR was at chance level (ROC-AUC \num{0.450}, $\mathrm{EF}_{5\,\%} = \num{0.00}$); (ii) rigid $\Delta\Delta G$ did not track curated ChEMBL genotype-resolved fold-shifts (28 paired compounds; $\rho$ \numrange{-0.13}{0.19}, agreement \qtyrange{28.6}{41.7}{\percent}); (iii) RRS, PNS, and ACSI were mutually non-associated ($\rho \in [-0.41, -0.21]$, adjusted $p > 0.50$) and are reported as separate dimensions. To our knowledge, contemporaneous docking--MD antimalarial studies report neither a concordance quantification nor comparable negative controls. A claim-ledger table and practical-guidance paragraph assign each central claim its evidential status.

The main contributions of this work include:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.2em]
  \item \textit{Estimand divergence} between rigid docking retention and dynamic pocket relaxation as a protocol-local triage observation (7/8 matched states, single \qty{10}{\ns} replicate scope).
  \item Protocol-stability check on a 39-compound panel (\num{312} complexes) with class-level agreement under GNINA CNN rescoring (no rank transfer claimed).
  \item An explicit claim ledger with evidential status per claim --- including two negative controls --- plus four operational triage rules.
\end{enumerate}

We believe this calibration-forward treatment of docking--MD disagreement falls squarely within the methodological scope of \textit{JCIM}.

All code, configuration files, and datasets are publicly archived on Zenodo at \href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22829031}{DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22829031}. This manuscript is original, has not been submitted elsewhere, and has been approved by all co-authors.
