## Scientific boundaries

- The primary estimand is docking-derived RRS on the selected Set-C cohort.
- The primary target-balanced analysis contains 12 complete two-target candidates; five PfCRT-only candidates are sensitivity records.
- The 16-system Set-C MD pilot is single-replicate and secondary.
- MD-RRS$_d$ is a trajectory-geometry ratio, not affinity or resistance.
- MM-GBSA values are single-trajectory endpoint diagnostics, not converged free energies.
- The docking–MD direction comparison is restricted to the eight rows with a docking WT reference; seven of eight are divergent.
- Full-panel Set-C MD-RRS remains `NOT_COMPUTED` by design.
- M1 results are not integrated until production, QC, provenance reconciliation, and DAR update are complete.
- No biological target engagement, polypharmacology, clinical efficacy, or resistance circumvention is claimed.

## V2609 secondary RRS-margin analysis

The analysis uses the frozen `results/c_rrs_classification.csv`, thresholds 70% and 80%, and a ±5 percentage-point near-threshold flag. It produced 17 candidate summaries and 164 finite observations. It does not alter canonical classes or provide biological calibration.
