\subsection*{Convergence and interaction analysis}
All eighteen MM--GBSA endpoints (GB\textsuperscript{OBC2} igb=5, \qty{0.15}{\molar} salt, \num{100} snapshots each; seventeen cohort systems plus one independent PP-01 PfCRT WT replicate) converge with a within-trajectory SEM below \qty{1.0}{\kcalmol} (range \qty{0.25}{--}\qty{0.61}{\kcalmol}; e.g.\ PP-01 PfCRT WT \qty{-30.61\pm0.41}{\kcalmol}). Because snapshots are temporally autocorrelated, this SEM characterises sampling within a single trajectory and does not capture inter-replicate variability. The empirical noise floor of \qty{2.0}{\kcalmol} estimated from the two independent WT replicates (Table~S10) is therefore retained for interpreting mutant--wild-type contrasts.

Interaction fingerprints (ProLIF 2.2.1, \num{100} frames per trajectory, sixteen systems) show a sparse set of persistent contacts: four to eleven contacts per system exceed \qty{50}{\percent} occupancy, most at \qty{100}{\percent}. Recurring anchors include PfCRT TYR16 (fully occupied in five of six PfCRT systems) and PfDHFR LEU46/MET55, consistent with the canonical binding pockets. As a feasibility probe independent of the primary cohort, the single ligand PP-15 was docked to the wild-type states of PfCRT and PfDHFR (Vina 1.2.7) and prepared under the identical OpenFF~2.2.0/CHARMM36m explicit-solvent protocol used for the Set-C pilot. Both systems passed the pre-equilibration audits and completed EM--NVT--NPT equilibration and \qty{10}{ns} production trajectories with valid checkpoints (\cref{tab:s_pp15}); trajectory QC (heavy-atom protein--ligand minimum distance \num{< 5.0} \si{\angstrom} for \num{>= 10}\% of frames) passes for both, with a bound fraction of \num{1.000} (mean minimum distances \qty{2.77}{\angstrom} PfDHFR and \qty{3.20}{\angstrom} PfCRT). MM-GBSA estimates on PBC-whole trajectories (GB\textsuperscript{OBC2} igb=5, \qty{0.15}{\molar} salt) are \qty{-29.52 \pm 0.33}{\kcalmol} (PfDHFR) and \qty{-27.79 \pm 0.49}{\kcalmol} (PfCRT), and are reported in \cref{tab:s_pp15}. The probe is outside the 17-candidate Set-C estimand: no mutant or RRS estimate is reported for PP-15, and the single-replicate endpoints serve only as within-protocol feasibility contrasts. Docking-mode reproducibility for both PP-15 and PP-01 was checked across five random seeds (\cref{tab:s17_multiseed_redock}). Mode-1 affinity is stable to within \qty{0.03}{\kcalmol} for PP-15 and \qty{0.05}{\kcalmol} for PP-01 on both wild-type receptors, so the seed choice does not drive the probe ranking for either candidate.
