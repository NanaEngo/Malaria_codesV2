\subsection*{Complete ACSI components}

ACSI was calculated as
\[
\mathrm{ACSI}=0.40D_{\mathrm{DrugBank}}+0.25D_{\mathrm{ANPDB}}+0.20f_{sp^3}+0.15\mathrm{NPL}.
\]
Components were min--max normalized over the 17-candidate cohort. Two candidates exceeded ACSI $>\num{0.70}$, and the cohort mean was \num{0.543}. Varying one weight at a time by $\pm\qty{20}{\percent}$ gave Spearman correlations of \numrange{0.9167}{0.9804} and top-five Jaccard overlaps of \numrange{0.6667}{1.0000}.

\subsection*{DiffDock--Vina and activity-model comparisons}

In the 65\,856-molecule library, Vina\citep{vina_2021} scores and DiffDock\citep{diffdock_2023} confidence were only weakly related overall. A Benjamini--Hochberg analysis of \num{18} pairwise activity comparisons across compound groups using three Ersilia\citep{ersilia_2023} models found no adjusted-significant differences; all rank-biserial effect sizes were negligible. These analyses provide no evidence of biological activity.
