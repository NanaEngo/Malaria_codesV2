\subsection*{Complete ACSI components}

ACSI was calculated as
\[
\mathrm{ACSI}=0.40D_{\mathrm{DrugBank}}+0.25D_{\mathrm{ANPDB}}+0.20f_{sp^3}+0.15\mathrm{NPL}.
\]
Components were min--max normalized over the selected 17-candidate cohort. Two candidates exceeded 0.70, the cohort mean was 0.543, and the complete component values are retained in the machine-readable source table. The eight one-at-a-time $\pm20\%$ perturbations gave Spearman rank correlations of 0.9167--0.9804 and top-five Jaccard overlaps of 0.6667--1.0000.
