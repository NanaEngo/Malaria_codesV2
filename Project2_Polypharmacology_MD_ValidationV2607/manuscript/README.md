## 📊 Manuscript Structure

### Abstract (250 words)
- **Background**: Antimalarial resistance challenge
- **Methods**: MD validation of polypharmacological candidates
- **Results**: Key findings and metrics
- **Conclusions**: Implications for drug discovery

### Introduction (~1,500 words)
- **Problem statement**: Antimalarial resistance
- **Current approaches**: Limitations of single-target drugs
- **Polypharmacology**: Advantages and challenges
- **Computational validation**: MD simulation approach
- **Study objectives**: Specific aims and hypotheses

### Methods (~2,000 words)
- **Candidate selection**: Refined MPO scoring and filtering
- **System preparation**: Protein and ligand preparation
- **MD simulations**: Protocol and parameters
- **Analysis methods**: Novel metrics (RRS, ACSI, PNS)
- **Statistical analysis**: Validation and significance testing

### Results (~2,500 words)
- **Candidate selection**: 20 compounds from 93 candidates
- **MD simulation results**: Stability and binding analysis
- **Resistance assessment**: Performance against mutants
- **Polypharmacology evaluation**: Multi-target activity
- **Novel metrics**: RRS, ACSI, PNS validation

### Discussion (~1,500 words)
- **Key findings**: Interpretation of results
- **Comparison with literature**: Context and significance
- **Limitations**: Study constraints and assumptions
- **Future directions**: Experimental validation needs

### Conclusions (~500 words)
- **Summary**: Main contributions
- **Implications**: Drug discovery impact
- **Recommendations**: Next steps
