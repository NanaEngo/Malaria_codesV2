CC   -!- POLYMORPHISM: The 7G8 strain is chloroquine-resistant in contrast to
CC       the 3D7 strain which is chloroquine-sensitive. Compared to the 3D7
CC       strain, the CRT protein from the 7G8 strain shows increased chloroquine
CC       transport rates and reduced capacity for the transport of natural
CC       substrates (PubMed:25733858, PubMed:31776516, PubMed:32764664). In
CC       contrast to the CRT protein from 3D7 strain that does not transport
CC       lumefantrine or mefloquine, the CRT protein from the 7G8 strain
CC       exhibits significant transport activity for both drugs
CC       (PubMed:35507548). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:25733858,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:31776516, ECO:0000269|PubMed:32764664,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:35507548}.
CC   -!- MISCELLANEOUS: Can function as a drug transporter (PubMed:25733858,
CC       PubMed:31776516, PubMed:32764664). Can transport chloroquine and
CC       verapamil (PubMed:25733858). Active transport of chloroquine and
CC       arginine requires a membrane potential and a directed pH gradient:
CC       5.5->7.5, corresponding to the digestive vacuole pH gradient of the
CC       parasite (PubMed:25733858, PubMed:31776516). Binding with chloroquine
CC       and piperaquine is inhibited by verapamil, amodiaquine and excess
CC       arginine (PubMed:31776516). Can transport lumefantrine or mefloquine
CC       (PubMed:35507548). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:25733858,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:31776516, ECO:0000269|PubMed:32764664,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:35507548}.
