## Scientific mitigations applied

1. **MMV/DEKOIS distinction strengthened.** The abstract, main text, SM enrichment table, and SM detailed analysis explicitly distinguish score-stratified MMV retrodictive consistency from independent DEKOIS decoy discrimination.
2. **DiffDock claim bounded.** The text now states that the DEKOIS result motivates, but does not isolate or independently establish, a DiffDock contribution because the full consensus was not evaluated on the same DEKOIS panel.
3. **False-positive language corrected.** Consensus hit-rate changes are described as computational candidate-pool narrowing, not measured false-positive reduction; an independent inactive panel remains required.
4. **4GM2 identity retained and clarified.** 4GM2 is reported as PfClpR-specific and never as PfClpP validation.
5. **SI$_{pred}$ framing retained as heuristic.** Selectivity, activity, ADMET, docking, synthesisability, and lead counts remain explicitly computational and require experimental confirmation.
6. **Residual overstatement reduced.** SM captions and notes no longer treat low correlation as proof of independence, pose quality, biological relevance, or crystal-ligand validity.
7. **Cross-reference warnings fixed.** Prefixed external-document section references that generated untyped `cleveref` warnings were replaced with explicit `hyperref` links to Supplementary Methods.
8. **Whitespace cleaned.** `git diff --check` passes for the P1 V4 manuscript package.
