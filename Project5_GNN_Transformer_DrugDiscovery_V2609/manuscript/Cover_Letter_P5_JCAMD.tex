\opening{Dear Editors,}

\noindent 13 September 2026

We submit the manuscript entitled \textbf{``Deconstructing Out-of-Distribution Extrapolation in Molecular Deep Learning: Structural Complexity, Topological Rescue, and Conformal Triage on African Antimalarial Natural Product Scaffolds''} for consideration as a Research Article in the \textit{Journal of Computer-Aided Molecular Design}.

Rather than offering another passive benchmark reporting fingerprint dominance, this study delivers a mechanistic explanation for graph neural network extrapolation failures and an actionable decision framework for computational virtual screening under out-of-distribution (OOD) shift. On a frozen panel of \num{19836} African antimalarial natural products and synthetic derivatives, evaluated across \num{25} fold--seed replicates per model, we establish three major contributions:

First, we demonstrate that standard GNN performance drops sharply on stereochemically complex 3D polycyclic scaffolds ($Fsp^3 \ge 0.45$, ring count $\ge 4$, ROC AUC dropping to \num{0.768}), exposing the theoretical 1-WL Weisfeiler--Lehman graph expressivity bottleneck. Fusing persistent-homology topological feature projections (GIN--TFP) injects global structural shape invariants ($H_0, H_1$), recovering up to \qty{36}{\percent} of the extrapolation gap (\num{0.815} ROC AUC, a $+\num{0.047}$ gain over base GIN).

Second, we convert these distance-resolved insights into an actionable Distance-Aware Conformal Triage Filter. By dynamically routing low-similarity candidates ($D_{\text{NN}} < 0.40$) to ECFP4--RF and mid-range candidates to GIN--TFP while monitoring calibration degradation ($\mathrm{ECE} > 0.08$), this triage protocol boosts prospective virtual screening precision by \textbf{+\qty{14.2}{\percent}} on a \num{22267}-molecule disjoint external ChEMBL transfer panel.

Third, we establish a normative benchmark standard by proving that fold-independent weight restoration in molecular sequence transformers is mandatory to prevent artificial cross-fold information leakage during cross-validation.

The work is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, has been approved by all authors, and the authors declare no competing financial interests.
