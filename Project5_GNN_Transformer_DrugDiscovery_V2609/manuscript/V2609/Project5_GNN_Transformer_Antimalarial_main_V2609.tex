\begin{abstract}
Graph neural networks (GNNs) and molecular transformers frequently underperform classical fingerprint-based tree ensembles under scaffold separation, yet the structural mechanics governing this extrapolation deficit remain poorly understood. On a frozen panel of \num{19836} African antimalarial natural products and synthetic derivatives---characterised by the African-Chemotype Structural Index (ACSI) and exhibiting a structural paradox of \qty{92.6}{\percent} whole-molecule novelty yet \qty{69.3}{\percent} conservation of privileged African ring cores---derived from the African Natural Products Database (ANPDB / AfroDb), we deconstruct representation performance across \num{25} fold--seed replicates per model using a distance-resolved Molecular Out-of-Distribution (MOOD) framework. Under Bemis--Murcko scaffold separation, ECFP4--RF reached mean test ROC AUC \num{0.8300}, statistically outperforming GIN--TFP (\num{0.8138}), GIN--TNE (\num{0.8090}), GIN (\num{0.8047}) and ChemBERTa (\num{0.7867}) after Benjamini--Hochberg correction. Nearest-training-neighbour Tanimoto decile stratification reveals that the ECFP4 advantage is strictly localized in the structural extrapolation tail (deciles D1--D4, NN-similarity $< 0.40$, $\Delta \in \numrange{0.016}{0.027}$), whereas deep models achieve near-parity in mid-range interpolation (D5--D8, $\Delta \in \numrange{0.001}{0.007}$). Fusing multi-scale persistent-homology images into a graph isomorphism network (GIN--TFP) recovers up to \qty{36}{\percent} of the extrapolation gap, overcoming the 1-WL expressivity bottleneck on complex cyclic natural product scaffolds ($Fsp^3 \ge 0.45$, ring count $\ge 4$). To exploit these dynamics prospectively, we formulate a Distance-Aware Conformal Triage Filter combining Tanimoto distance with expected calibration error. This decision rule dynamically routes low-similarity OOD candidates to ECFP4--RF and mid-range candidates to GIN--TFP, increasing virtual screening precision by \qty{14.2}{\percent} over single-model baselines. Finally, we demonstrate that fold-independent weight restoration in transformers is essential to eliminate cross-fold information leakage. This work establishes a mechanistic and operational foundation for molecular representation deployment under chemical distribution shift.
\end{abstract}

\end{itemize}

\section{Introduction}

The parasite is winning. Artemisinin-resistant \emph{Plasmodium falciparum} strains, first documented in Southeast Asia, have now been confirmed across sub-Saharan Africa --- the region that bears 94\% of the \num{608000} malaria deaths recorded in \num{2023} \citep{who2024malariareport,ariyey2014,ashley2018}. The pipeline of synthetic antimalarials has not kept pace. African medicinal plants have: \emph{Cryptolepis sanguinolenta}, \emph{Enantia chlorantha}, and \emph{Nauclea latifolia} produce indoloquinoline alkaloids, protoberberine scaffolds, and quassinoids shaped by millions of years of host--parasite co-evolution into structures no combinatorial library has replicated.

Screening this chemical space computationally reveals an immediate structural paradox. The \num{19836}-molecule panel examined here --- derived from \num{396} African natural products in ANPDB/AfroDb \citep{anpdb_2026} and expanded into a library of antimalarial candidates \citep{temgoua2026antimalarial} --- exhibits \qty{92.6}{\percent} whole-molecule novelty relative to standard synthetic screening libraries (Tanimoto $< 0.40$), yet retains \qty{69.3}{\percent} of privileged African ring cores, quantified by the African-Chemotype Structural Index (ACSI). The molecules are globally new; their scaffolds are not. This tension is not a curiosity: it is the structural signature that makes this panel a precise stress-test for molecular representation learning.

The structural density of these scaffolds --- elevated $F_{\mathrm{sp3}} \ge 0.45$, fused polycyclic cores, multiple stereocenters --- is precisely what breaks graph neural networks under scaffold separation. Standard message-passing architectures are bounded by the 1-Weisfeiler-Lehman (1-WL) expressivity test: they aggregate local atomic neighbourhoods but cannot resolve higher-order topological motifs such as bridged polycyclic cages or macrocyclic ring systems. On flat aromatic synthetics, this limitation is largely invisible. On African natural product scaffolds, it is the dominant failure mode.

The gap between fingerprint baselines and learned representations is not uniform across chemical space --- it is a function of structural distance from the training distribution. Nearest-neighbour Tanimoto decile stratification reveals that ECFP4--RF dominates only in the cold extrapolation tail ($D_{\mathrm{NN}} < 0.40$, deciles D1--D4), while deep models achieve near-parity in mid-range interpolation (D5--D8, $\Delta \in [0.001, 0.007]$). This distance-resolved picture, absent from prior benchmarks \citep{guo_ding_2026,benchmark_pretrained_2025}, is the analytical core of the Molecular Out-of-Distribution (MOOD) framework introduced here.

Fusing multi-scale persistent-homology images into a graph isomorphism network (GIN--TFP) injects global topological invariants --- zero-dimensional connectivity ($H_0$) and one-dimensional cycle persistence ($H_1$) --- that operate independently of local message passing. On polycyclic scaffolds (Rings $\ge 4$), this topological rescue recovers \qty{73}{\percent} of the cohort-level GNN deficit; at the aggregate scaffold split, it closes \qty{36}{\percent} of the gap. To exploit this distance-dependence prospectively, we formulate a Distance-Aware Conformal Triage Filter: a decision rule that routes each candidate to ECFP4--RF ($D_{\mathrm{NN}} < 0.40$) or GIN--TFP ($0.40 \le D_{\mathrm{NN}} \le 0.60$) based on its structural distance and expected calibration error, delivering a \qty{+14.2}{\percent} precision gain on the \num{22267}-compound ChEMBL transfer panel.

A fourth methodological contribution concerns molecular transformers specifically: enforcing per-fold pretrained weight restoration eliminates cross-fold information leakage that artificially inflates ChemBERTa performance estimates during cross-validation. Together, these four contributions --- distance-resolved mechanics, topological rescue, conformal triage, and leakage-free evaluation --- are operationalised through a centroid-based screening reduction that cuts compute overhead by \qty{99.3}{\percent} (\num{263000} to \num{1936} representative evaluations), making the full pipeline deployable on the HPC infrastructure available to malaria-endemic African research institutions without dependence on commercial cloud resources.

The central question this paper addresses is: \emph{under chemical-distribution shift, how do structural complexity and representation topology dictate the out-of-distribution extrapolation limits of molecular neural networks versus sparse bit-ensembles, and can distance-aware uncertainty triage dynamically optimise virtual screening performance?} Random forests trained on ECFP fingerprints remain highly competitive on datasets of a few thousand molecules, and gains reported for GCN, GAT, or GIN architectures on large curated benchmarks often diminish significantly under scaffold splits \citep{guo_ding_2026,wu_molnet_2018,benchmark_pretrained_2025}. For natural products specifically, a \num{20}-fingerprint benchmark across $>\num{100000}$ compounds confirmed that no single encoding dominates \citep{boldini2024}. Topological data analysis introduces multi-scale geometric invariants through persistent homology \citep{tda_review_2025}, and tensor-network embeddings compress scaffold representations via Tucker decomposition \citep{tn_efficient_2026}; neither family has been systematically evaluated inside a graph fusion architecture under rigorous scaffold-held-out testing on an antimalarial natural product panel --- the gap this study closes.

\subsection{Distance-Aware Conformal Triage Filter}
\label{sec:conformal-triage}

Deploying a single model uniformly across chemical space is not neutral --- it is a systematic error. In the cold OOD tail ($D_{\text{NN}} < 0.40$, deciles D1--D4), GIN--TFP underperforms ECFP4--RF by up to \num{0.027} AUC points; in the interpolation regime (D5--D8), the positions reverse. The Conformal Triage Filter exploits this asymmetry by routing each candidate according to its nearest-training-neighbour Tanimoto distance and expected calibration error (Eq.~\ref{eq:conformal_triage}); when batch-level confidence breaches $\mathrm{ECE} > 0.08$, Platt scaling is triggered prior to routing. Evaluated on the \num{22267} molecule-disjoint ChEMBL transfer panel, this yields $+\qty{14.2}{\percent}$ top-$k$ precision ($k=500$) over the best single-model baseline.

\subsection{Topological descriptor salience}

Topological features are not ignored by the network: across \num{25} replicates, the persistent-image block (dimensions \num{33}--\num{57}) consistently dominates the TFP fusion head (mean weight \num{0.0790} vs.\ \num{0.0485} for $H_0$ and \num{0.0547} for Betti), confirming that $H_1$ cycle persistence --- the geometric signature of fused ring systems --- is the active ingredient of the topological rescue. Yet aggregate scaffold-split AUC gains remain modest (\num{0.0091} points): topological invariants rescue specific cohorts --- polycyclic and complex 3D scaffolds --- not the full distribution. Salience is necessary but not sufficient; the gain is structurally localised (\cref{fig:salience_weights}).

\section{Discussion}

\subsection{The 1-WL expressivity bottleneck on African natural product scaffolds}

African natural product scaffolds do not merely challenge molecular representations --- they expose their theoretical limits. The 1-WL test cannot distinguish bridged polycyclic cages from their planar projections; on a panel where \qty{18.4}{\percent} of molecules carry $Fsp^3 \ge 0.45$, this blind spot is not marginal. Indoloquinoline alkaloids (cryptolepine, isocryptolepine) from \emph{Cryptolepis sanguinolenta}, protoberberine and quinoline alkaloids from \emph{Enantia chlorantha} and \emph{Nauclea latifolia}, quassinoids, and prenylated chromones all present bridged polycyclic frameworks and multiple stereocenters that local message aggregation cannot resolve. In these regimes, over-smoothing is a biophysical inevitability: the scaffold geometry exceeds what pairwise neighbourhood aggregation can encode, regardless of depth or width.

Two independent lines of evidence confirm that this ceiling is architectural, not a tuning artefact. First, the capacity sweep (SLURM Job 15617) bounds scaffold AUC in $[0.8000, 0.8081]$ across $h \in \{64, 128, 256\}$ and dropout $p \in \{0.1, 0.2\}$ across \num{25} fold--seed replicates (\cref{SM-sec:gnn_sensitivity_si}): adding parameters does not recover what the expressivity limit forbids. Second, the TFP rescue closes the argument from the opposite direction --- if the deficit were a data artefact or capacity gap, injecting persistent-homology invariants ($H_0$, $H_1$) would not selectively recover polycyclic cohorts. It does, and the gain scales monotonically with structural complexity ($+\num{0.007}$ flat aromatics, $+\num{0.038}$ complex 3D, $+\num{0.047}$ polycyclics), confirming that global topological information is precisely what 1-WL message passing cannot supply.

\subsection{Distance-resolved triage as operational consequence}

Uniform model deployment is a hidden assumption that the decile data invalidates. ECFP4--RF leads by \num{0.027} AUC at D1 and trails by \num{0.004} at D7: the same model cannot be optimal across this range. The $+\qty{14.2}{\percent}$ precision gain is not a bonus --- it is the cost of ignoring the asymmetry. The triage filter is therefore not a heuristic patch but the logical consequence of the distance-resolved picture: if model superiority is a function of $D_{\text{NN}}$ rather than a global property, routing by $D_{\text{NN}}$ is the only architecturally honest deployment strategy.

Distance routing alone is insufficient when probability estimates are unreliable. ECE doubles from \num{0.03} in-domain to $>\num{0.12}$ in the OOD tail: a model that routes correctly but scores incorrectly still misleads the medicinal chemist. The $\mathrm{ECE} > 0.08$ recalibration trigger is therefore not optional --- it is the condition under which the triage gain is valid. Platt scaling or isotonic regression must be applied before any hard-cutoff decision whenever query batches breach this threshold.

\subsection{Position relative to the representation benchmark literature}

Prior benchmarks established that fingerprints are competitive; none identified where or why. The MOOD framework supplies both: the $D_{\text{NN}} < 0.40$ boundary is the precise locus of ECFP4 superiority, and the 1-WL bottleneck is its mechanism. That this boundary holds invariant across five partition families --- random, scaffold, Butina, novel scaffold subsets, and decile stratification --- rules out partition geometry as a confound and establishes the result as a structural property of the panel, not an artefact of evaluation design.

These findings converge with and sharpen recent systematic evaluations. Guo and Ding's fingerprint dominance in \qty{47.4}{\percent} of tasks \citep{guo_ding_2026} and the 25-embedding survey's null neural advantage over ECFP \citep{benchmark_pretrained_2025} are aggregate signals whose mechanistic origin the present study resolves: the advantage is distance-localised, not global. Where Boldini et al.\ found no dominant encoding across $>\num{100000}$ natural products \citep{boldini2024}, we identify the structural regime --- $Fsp^3 \ge 0.45$, polycyclic --- where topological fusion provides genuine, reproducible complementary value. The leakage-free transformer protocol adds a methodological dimension absent from all prior surveys: fold-independent weight restoration is a prerequisite for valid cross-validation estimates on pretrained sequence models.

\subsection{Limitations and computational sovereignty}

Three boundaries constrain the generalisability of these findings. The panel is restricted to antiplasmodial activity; cellular phenotype profiles (LISH/MoA) involve transcriptomic readouts that fingerprint locality and topological invariants do not govern, and the molecular--phenotypic boundary is explicitly maintained throughout (\cref{SM-sec:lish_si}). The triage precision gain of $+\qty{14.2}{\percent}$ is measured on a single ChEMBL transfer panel; validation on independent prospective screens remains outstanding. And the TFP rescue is cohort-specific: on flat aromatic synthetics, persistent homology adds \num{0.007} AUC points --- a gain indistinguishable from noise at the aggregate level.

A constraint that is often treated as peripheral is here central: the entire pipeline must be deployable without commercial cloud infrastructure. The panel that makes the triage filter necessary --- high-$Fsp^3$, polycyclic, structurally novel --- is precisely the panel that most African institutions cannot afford to screen at scale without the compute reduction it enables. The centroid-based screening reduction (\num{263000} to \num{1936} representative evaluations, \qty{99.3}{\percent} overhead reduction) is therefore not a convenience optimisation; it is the condition under which autonomous drug discovery capacity exists in malaria-endemic African research institutions.

\section{Conclusions}

Model choice in virtual screening is not a global decision --- it is a spatial, distance-dependent choice. The MOOD framework demonstrates that ECFP4--RF superiority is strictly confined to the structural extrapolation tail ($D_{\text{NN}} < 0.40$), that the 1-Weisfeiler--Lehman (1-WL) expressivity ceiling on high-$F_{\mathrm{sp3}}$ polycyclic scaffolds is its biophysical mechanism, and that multiscale topological fusion selectively rescues the exact structural regime where message-passing graph neural networks over-smooth.

Two methodological standards emerge from this work. The Conformal Triage Filter formalises distance-dependent routing as the only honest deployment strategy when model superiority is spatially heterogeneous, yielding $+\qty{14.2}{\percent}$ top-$k$ precision ($k=500$) on the \num{22267}-compound ChEMBL transfer panel. Fold-independent weight restoration in ChemBERTa eliminates the cross-fold leakage that inflates transformer benchmarks --- a prerequisite, not an optimisation.

The \qty{99.3}{\percent} compute reduction is not a performance footnote --- it is the condition under which this framework is usable by the institutions that need it most. Centroid-based screening (\num{263000} to \num{1936} evaluations) brings multi-target virtual screening of African natural product libraries within reach of HPC infrastructure in malaria-endemic research institutions, without dependence on commercial cloud resources. What remains is biology: triage-selected candidates must now face drug-resistant \emph{Plasmodium falciparum} isolates in vitro --- the only test that converts a ranked list into an antimalarial lead.

\section{Methods}

\subsection{Data curation and structural descriptors}

We evaluate representations on a frozen panel of \num{19836} antimalarial molecules curated from the eos80ch collection \citep{temgoua2026antimalarial}, originating from African Natural Product Database (ANPDB) seeds and synthetic analogues. Molecular representations comprise \num{2048}-bit ECFP4 Morgan fingerprints (radius 2), \num{78}-dimensional topological feature projections (TFP), and \num{192}-dimensional tensor-network embeddings (TNE). Structural complexity metrics, including the fraction of $sp^3$-hybridised carbons ($F_{\mathrm{sp3}}$), ring architecture counts, and scaffold entropy, were computed using RDKit \citep{RDKit}.

\subsection{Partition families and statistical evaluation protocol}

Model generalisation was stress-tested across four distinct partition families:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Stratified random split.} Uniform 80/10/10 partitioning serving as the in-domain baseline.
    \item \textbf{Canonical scaffold split.} Bemis--Murcko ring-core separation \citep{bemis_murcko_1996} enforcing structural extrapolation.
    \item \textbf{Butina cluster split.} Hierarchical clustering based on ECFP4 Tanimoto distance with a similarity cutoff of $C=0.55$.
    \item \textbf{$NN$-Tanimoto decile stratification.} Test candidate partition into deciles D1--D10 ordered by nearest-neighbour similarity to the training set.
\end{enumerate}
All evaluation protocols adhere to a repeated $5 \times 5$ cross-validation design (\num{25} fold--seed execution records per model architecture and partition scheme). Paired hypothesis testing operates on the five per-seed performance means ($df=4$) with Benjamini--Hochberg false discovery rate adjustment ($p < 0.001$).

\subsection{Model architectures and leakage-free transformer initialisation}

Five primary architectures were benchmarked:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{ECFP4--RF.} Random Forest classifier (\num{500} trees) trained on \num{2048}-bit ECFP4 fingerprints.
    \item \textbf{GIN.} 3-layer Graph Isomorphism Network (\num{128} hidden units, dropout $p=0.1$, ReLU activations, batch normalisation after each message-passing layer), bounded by 1-Weisfeiler--Lehman (1-WL) expressivity limits.
    \item \textbf{GIN--TFP.} Multiscale hybrid architecture concatenating the GIN graph embedding with a \num{78}-dimensional persistent-homology feature vector ($H_0$ connectivity persistence: dims \num{0}--\num{32}; $H_1$ cycle persistence: dims \num{33}--\num{57}; scalar Betti invariants: dims \num{58}--\num{77}) prior to the classification head.
    \item \textbf{GIN--TNE.} Hybrid architecture replacing the TFP block with a \num{192}-dimensional Tucker-decomposed tensor-network embedding.
    \item \textbf{ChemBERTa.} Pretrained sequence transformer (\texttt{seyonec/ChemBERTa-zinc-base-v1}) fine-tuned on canonical SMILES strings. To eliminate cross-fold information leakage, pretrained weights were fully reset to their initial state prior to each cross-validation fold.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Distance-aware conformal triage formulation}

The Conformal Triage Filter evaluates a query candidate molecule $x$ against training library $\mathcal{T}$ by calculating its nearest-neighbour Tanimoto similarity $D_{\text{NN}}(x) = \max_{y \in \mathcal{T}} s_{\mathrm{Tanimoto}}(x, y)$ and batch Expected Calibration Error $\mathrm{ECE}(x)$. Candidates are dynamically routed according to the decision rule:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:conformal_triage}
\mathcal{M}(x) = 
\begin{cases}
\text{ECFP4--RF}, & D_{\text{NN}}(x) < 0.40 \quad \text{(Cold OOD Extrapolation Tail)}, \\
\text{GIN--TFP}, & 0.40 \le D_{\text{NN}}(x) \le 0.60 \quad \text{(Familiar Chemistry Regime)}, \\
\text{Ensemble Consensus}, & D_{\text{NN}}(x) > 0.60 \quad \text{(High-Similarity Core)}.
\end{cases}
\end{equation}
When batch confidence degradation breaches the threshold $\mathrm{ECE} > 0.08$, post-hoc Platt scaling or isotonic regression is triggered prior to candidate ranking. The complete, executable Python implementation listing is available in Supporting Information (\cref{SM-sec:triage_code_si}).
