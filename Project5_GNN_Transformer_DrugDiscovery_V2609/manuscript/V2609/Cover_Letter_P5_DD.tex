\opening{Dear Editors,}

\noindent 16 September 2026

We submit the manuscript entitled \textbf{``Out-of-distribution extrapolation in molecular deep learning: topological rescue and conformal triage for antimalarial scaffolds''} for consideration as a Full Paper in \textit{Digital Discovery}.

This study speaks directly to your journal's data-first mission. Rather than another passive benchmark reporting fingerprint dominance, we deliver a mechanistic explanation for graph neural network extrapolation failures and an actionable decision framework for virtual screening under distribution shift. On a frozen panel of \num{19836} African antimalarial natural products and synthetic derivatives, evaluated across \num{25} fold--seed replicates per model, we establish three contributions:

First, we localise the ECFP4--RF advantage strictly to the structural extrapolation tail (nearest-neighbour similarity $< 0.40$) and identify its mechanism: the 1-Weisfeiler--Lehman expressivity ceiling of message-passing networks on high-$Fsp^3$ polycyclic scaffolds. Fusing persistent-homology topological features (GIN--TFP) recovers up to \qty{36}{\percent} of that gap.

Second, we operationalise these dynamics as a Distance-Aware Conformal Triage Filter that routes candidates to the right model by distance regime, improving prospective screening precision by \textbf{+\qty{14.2}{\percent}} on a disjoint \num{22267}-molecule ChEMBL transfer panel.

Third, we show that fold-independent weight restoration in molecular transformers is mandatory to prevent cross-fold leakage --- a reproducibility standard for the community.

\textbf{Data and code:} all splits, predictions, and analysis scripts are archived on Zenodo (DOI \href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19608875}{10.5281/zenodo.19608875}, record reserved pending upload completion) and GitHub (\url{https://github.com/taamangtchu/Project5_GNN_Transformer_DrugDiscovery}). Editors and referees will be given full access for peer review, and the DOI will be published before acceptance, per your data policy.

The work is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, has been approved by all authors, and there are no conflicts to declare. A TOC graphic (\texttt{figures/graphical\_abstract.jpg}) is uploaded alongside the manuscript files.
